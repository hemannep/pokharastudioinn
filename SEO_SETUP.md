# SEO launch checklist for pokharastudioinn.com.np

The site files already contain the canonical URL, metadata, structured data,
`robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`. Complete these steps immediately after upload:

1. Serve the site at `https://pokharastudioinn.com.np/` with a valid certificate.
2. Redirect every HTTP and `www` variant permanently to that exact HTTPS URL.
3. Add a Domain property for `pokharastudioinn.com.np` in Google Search Console.
4. Submit `https://pokharastudioinn.com.np/sitemap.xml` in Search Console.
5. Inspect the homepage URL and request indexing after the first deployment.
6. Claim or update the Google Business Profile using the same name, phone,
   address, map location, website URL, photos, and accommodation category.
7. Keep Airbnb and Google Business details consistent with the website.
8. Ask real guests for Google reviews; never buy or fabricate reviews.
9. Add new, genuinely useful local content over time (Lakeside guidance,
   long-stay information, transport, trekking luggage storage, and FAQs).
10. Test the live URL with Google Rich Results Test and PageSpeed Insights.

Search rankings cannot be guaranteed. Strong local rankings depend on the
published site, crawlability, Google Business Profile quality, authentic
reviews, relevant links, useful content, and time.
